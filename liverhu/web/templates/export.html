    How outliers are found

    Each scan is compared only with scans of the same type (non-contrast or contrast-enhanced), because contrast raises liver HU by tens of HU. In a group of at least 5 scans, a liver mean is an outlier when it lies more than 1.5 times the spread of the middle half of the scans below or above that middle half (Tukey's rule; the shaded band on the chart). The spreadsheet has the same mark in its liver_mean_outlier column.
